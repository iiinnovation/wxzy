## Summary Semantics

- Today uses the Owner IANA timezone and converts local midnight boundaries to UTC.
- Study days are distinct local dates containing a ReviewAttempt or a completed/interrupted session with actual minutes.
- New count comes from the immutable attempt `state_before.state == new` snapshot.
- Due means an active enrollment whose state is due at or before now; backlog is due before today's local midnight.
- Subject trend compares two 15-day windows. Each side needs at least two attempts before a direction is emitted.

## Separate Progress Measures

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Content covered | unique terminal chunk pages in each document's latest version |
| Cards published | catalog cards with `status=published` |
| Cards enrolled | Owner enrollments excluding retired |
| Cards active | Owner enrollments with `status=active` |
| Cards mastered | active review state, review phase, reps at least 3, latest Good/Easy, and not currently due |

These values are returned separately. Processing coverage is never presented as memory mastery.

## Workload

- Seven buckets start at the Owner's local today.
- Overdue states are placed in today's bucket; future states use their local due date.
- Review duration uses the latest 50 `response_ms` values and their median after at least five samples; cold start uses 45 seconds.
- Daily budget respects `LearningProfile.daily_minutes` and weekday flags.

## Weak Topics

Weak topics reuse P6-T06's deterministic suggestion builder. Results retain the stable severity, recent-failure, and card-id ordering and expose `offset`, `limit`, `total`, and `has_more`. The 35-row acceptance fixture verifies stable replay and non-overlapping pages.

## Rebuildability

The v1 read models hold no independent mutable aggregate state. They derive from catalog, enrollment, review state, attempts, sessions, and issues, so they can be rebuilt without repair migrations or drift. At the current single-Owner scale they run synchronously; the same builders can back an asynchronous materialization job if volume requires it.
